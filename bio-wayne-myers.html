Wayne
Myers

Honolulu

Wayne Myers is a founding partner of Blanchet LLP. A trial-ready litigator and former senior federal prosecutor, Wayne represents companies, executives, professionals, and individuals in high-stakes litigation, complex civil disputes, government and grand jury investigations, white-collar matters, and internal investigations. He also handles select criminal matters.

Wayne held some of the highest positions in the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the District of Hawaii, including First Assistant U.S. Attorney, First Deputy U.S. Attorney, Criminal Chief, and Appellate Chief. His remit covered the full range of federal litigation: criminal prosecutions, civil litigation, appeals, sensitive investigations, and office operations. He worked regularly with Department leadership in Washington, federal agencies, law enforcement, the judiciary, and community stakeholders.

Before Hawaii, Wayne served as an Assistant U.S. Attorney in the Northern District of New York, where he was Chief of the Albany Branch Office, supervised Criminal Division AUSAs, managed a 40-person office, and built a nationally recognized cybercrime program.

Over the course of his career, Wayne has tried more than a dozen cases to verdict, supervised dozens more, personally handled hundreds of federal court hearings, and appeared in hundreds of grand jury proceedings. He knows the federal system from the inside: how prosecutors evaluate cases, how agents build investigations, how grand juries operate, where investigations and cases hit stress points, when Main Justice gets involved, and how federal judges assess contested facts and law.

Wayne’s experience spans national security and terrorism, financial crime, procurement fraud, health care fraud, public corruption, cybercrime, environmental crime, crimes against children, violent crime, firearms and explosives offenses, narcotics, and cryptocurrency. He has handled high-level national security and counterterrorism matters and held high-level federal security clearances.

Unusual among litigators, Wayne is genuinely fluent in the technology itself. He is comfortable in the details of digital forensics, networks, electronic evidence, cryptocurrency, and cyber investigations, not just the law that surrounds them. He has particular depth in digital evidence and surveillance, including the Stored Communications Act, ECPA, CFAA, search warrants, Fourth Amendment issues, and cross-border evidence collection.

Before joining the Department of Justice, Wayne practiced at Kirkland & Ellis LLP in Chicago and New York, where he handled complex commercial litigation, bankruptcy litigation, trade-secret matters, and high-stakes business disputes.

Wayne’s philosophy as a lawyer is simple: keep what matters, lose what does not, and know that telling the difference is the work. He is decisive under pressure and quick on his feet, as ready to make the call in a crisis as to make the argument in court.
