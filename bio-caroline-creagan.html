Caroline E. Creagan is a litigator who assists clients in civil disputes, including product liability and toxic tort litigation, and brings prior experience preparing and overseeing casework in public-sector and judicial settings.

Before entering private practice, Caroline served as a law clerk in the New York Attorney General's Law Enforcement Misconduct Investigative Office, a judicial intern for Judge Lawrence E. Kahn of the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of New York, and a law clerk with the Edward P. Swyer Justice Center. She also served as an executive editor of the Albany Law Review.

Caroline brings strong research, writing, and oral advocacy skills to the matters she handles. She works carefully through the record, supports briefing and discovery, and helps prepare matters for efficient resolution or trial.
