Why we built Blanchet LLP

Blanchet LLP was built around a simple idea: clients hire litigators to solve problems, not to create more process.

When the stakes are high, clients need lawyers who take ownership, move quickly, think clearly, and care about the outcome as much as they do.

That is the model we set out to build.

Elite trial pedigree. Broad disputes capability. Direct partner involvement from start to finish. And a closer connection between the client, the lawyers with the most judgment, and the work itself.

At many large firms, that connection gets diluted. Matters become more layered, more expensive, and harder to manage. The work can begin to serve the process instead of the client.

We wanted a different standard.

That is what Blanchet LLP was built to deliver: the sophistication of top-tier litigation, with more ownership, sharper judgment, and closer attention to how the work serves the client.
