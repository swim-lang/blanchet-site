Hannah E. Amundsen is a litigator who represents companies in commercial disputes, product liability and mass tort matters, toxic tort litigation, and cases involving life sciences and health effects.

She has experience in state and federal court and with the full lifecycle of a case, from pleadings and discovery through motion practice and trial preparation. Before beginning her law practice, Hannah worked in government and politics, experience that informs how she approaches matters shaped by public institutions, regulatory context, and competing constituencies.

Hannah brings a thoughtful, organized approach to complex records and evolving disputes. She focuses on understanding the facts early, keeping the work moving, and helping the team prepare each matter with the client's objective in view.
