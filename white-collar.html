05 / Approach

How Blanchet LLP
Approaches This Work

Government investigations and enforcement matters tend to develop in unpredictable ways. Early decisions about preservation, internal communications, witness coordination, and engagement with regulators often define everything that follows.


Blanchet LLP’s approach is direct and controlled. Senior lawyers stay close to the work, teams stay lean, and strategy is built around understanding the full landscape of the client’s exposure before recommending a course of action.

This means that being ready for hearings, negotiations, or trial (if the matter demands it) are all part of the initial strategy. Credibility with prosecutors, regulators, and opposing counsel comes from preparation, not posture.


Clients receive clear, honest assessments. Strategy is built around the facts as they are, not as anyone wishes they were. And communication stays consistent with the client, co-counsel, and the government.
