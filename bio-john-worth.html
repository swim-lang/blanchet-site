John
Worth

Chicago

John Worth is a veteran trial lawyer who represents clients in complex commercial disputes, class actions, environmental and toxic tort matters, product liability, consumer fraud, and energy-related litigation.

Before joining Blanchet LLP, John spent more than a decade as a litigation partner at Kirkland & Ellis and later led the launch of Phillips Lytle's Chicago office. He has tried and litigated cases in federal and state courts, appellate courts, and arbitrations across the country.

John is known for distilling complicated factual and legal records into a strategy that clients, courts, and decision-makers can understand.

REPRESENTATIVE EXPERIENCE

John Worth's experience includes:
