Frank G.
Dylewski

Chicago

Frank Dylewski is a litigator who represents companies, business owners, and individuals in commercial disputes, class and mass actions, product liability matters, partnership disputes, antitrust cases, insurance disputes, and other complex litigation.

His experience spans energy, finance, manufacturing, technology, insurance, and private equity. Before joining Blanchet, Frank practiced at global firms including Kirkland & Ellis and Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan. Having practiced on both sides of the "v." his experience with the heavyweight plaintiff's firm Keller Postman provides unique insight into how plaintiffs develop strategy and litigate cases.

Frank brings a practical, direct approach to disputes that require early case assessment, focused discovery including careful deposition work, and a clear view of the client's business objective. He is comfortable managing matters through motion practice, trial, arbitration, or negotiated resolution.
