[ SELECTED EXPERIENCE ]

Representative work across serious disputes.

The firm’s experience spans commercial litigation, environmental and toxic tort matters, product liability, securities, bankruptcy, IP, investigations, and appellate work.

Commercial Litigation / Insurance Recovery

OIL v. The Dow Chemical Company

Counsel to Dow in litigation involving a natural gas leak in Louisiana. The team secured summary judgment on more than $100 million in damages claims and achieved substantial counterclaim recoveries.

Mass Tort / Chemical Exposure

June v. Union Carbide Company

Counsel to Union Carbide in claims alleging mass bodily injury over a period of more than 50 years. Summary judgment or dismissal was secured for the claims of more than 200 plaintiffs, with the result affirmed by the Tenth Circuit.

Intellectual Property

Nilssen v. Osram Sylvania

Counsel to Osram Sylvania in a patent dispute where fifteen patents were held unenforceable and more than $6.1 million in fees and costs were awarded.

Class Action / Consumer Products

In re Horsepower Litigation

Counsel to Briggs & Stratton in nationwide multidistrict consumer-fraud class actions involving more than 50 proposed classes. The matters resolved favorably before discovery began.

Securities / M&A Litigation

Tenet v. Community Health Systems

Counsel in litigation following a hostile offer, resulting in complete dismissal of securities fraud and related claims.

Bankruptcy / Manufacturing

In re Tower Automotive

Counsel in Chapter 11 litigation involving efforts to reject collective bargaining agreements, resolved on favorable terms at completion of trial.

Our Approach

Every matter begins with the same discipline: understand the record, define the real problem, and build a strategy that fits the client’s objective.

STEP 1

Understand the Record

Start with the facts, the documents, the forum, and the client's objectives. Good strategy depends on a clear view of what is actually in front of you.

+Read the record closely
+Identify the real exposure
+Align on the objective
STEP 2

Set the Strategy

Decide early what the case demands, where leverage is likely to come from, and how the matter should be staffed and run.

+Early case assessment
+Focused staffing
+Clear path forward
STEP 3

Move the Matter

Move the case with discipline. Keep communication direct, staff leanly, and do the work that matters to the outcome.

+Lean teams
+Clear ownership
+Controlled execution
STEP 4

Stay Ready

Whether the matter resolves or goes to trial, preparation should create leverage at every stage.

+Strong preparation
+Better leverage
+Ready for trial
