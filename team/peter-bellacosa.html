[ GENERAL COUNSEL AND PARTNER ]

Peter A.
Bellacosa

New York City

Peter A. Bellacosa is General Counsel and Partner at Blanchet LLP. A trial and appellate lawyer with more than 30 years of experience, he represents companies and individuals in complex business disputes in state and federal courts, arbitrations, and regulatory matters.

As the Firm's inaugural General Counsel, Peter serves as the Firm's point of contact on internal ethics, compliance, and conflicts matters. He brings decades of experience handling legal ethics and compliance issues, internal investigations, and complex conflicts issues. While a partner at Kirkland & Ellis, Peter worked closely with the firm's lead Ethics & Compliance Partner and General Counsel in addressing and resolving internal firm compliance matters.

Peter has served as lead counsel in individual and class actions for clients across industries. His practice includes product liability and mass tort litigation, ERISA and employment disputes, commercial and contractual litigation, securities matters, New York State practice and procedure, and class action defense.

Peter has appeared in trial and appellate courts across the country, tried cases in court and arbitration, argued appeals in federal and state courts, and led teams of lawyers, local counsel, paralegals, experts, and support staff. He also brings significant pro bono and public-service experience, including his appointment to the Attorney Grievance Committee for the New York Supreme Court, Appellate Division, First Judicial Department, which oversees attorney disciplinary complaints, investigations, and proceedings involving attorneys practicing in Manhattan and the Bronx.

REPRESENTATIVE EXPERIENCE

Peter's experience includes:

  • Defending a chemical manufacturer in New York water districts' 1,4-dioxane litigation.
  • Representing a financial services firm in state court litigation involving breach of non-compete claims against a former managing director.
  • Defending a hedge fund and individuals in arbitration involving non-compete and non-solicitation claims.
  • Defending a leading property and casualty insurance company in ERISA, ADEA, civil RICO, breach of contract, tort, and employment discrimination matters.
  • Defending a leading investment bank in securities fraud class action litigation.
  • Defending major automobile companies in recalls, multidistrict litigation, and NHTSA and DOJ investigations concerning ignition switch and emissions issues.
  • Defending a major oil company in class action litigation involving MTBE groundwater contamination and in an investigation concerning the Deepwater Horizon incident.
  • Defending the tobacco industry in lawsuits brought by states, unions, individual smoker classes, and foreign governments.
